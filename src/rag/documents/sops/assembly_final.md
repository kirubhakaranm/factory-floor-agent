# SOP-ASM-03: Final Fit and Closure Installation

**Station:** ASM-03-FNL | **Machine:** ASM-03-FNL-RBT01  
**Revision:** 2.2 | **Effective:** 2025-09-20

## Purpose

Final mechanical assembly: doors, hood, trunk/hatch, fenders, bumpers, glass, wheels, suspension, brakes, and fluid fill.

## Installation Sequence

1. **Front/rear suspension** — pre-assembled modules, bolted to body (4 bolts each, 120 Nm)
2. **Brake system** — calipers, rotors, lines, fluid fill and bleed
3. **Wheels** — 5 lug nuts per wheel (140 Nm, star pattern)
4. **Doors** — hang on hinges (pre-aligned in body shop), adjust gap/flush
5. **Hood** — hang, adjust gap/flush/parallelism
6. **Trunk lid / hatchback** — hang, adjust, install gas struts
7. **Fenders** — bolt-on, adjust gap to door and hood
8. **Front/rear bumpers** — clip + bolt retention
9. **Windshield and rear glass** — adhesive bond (24hr full cure)
10. **Fluid fill** — brake fluid, coolant, washer fluid, gear oil

## Gap and Flush Specifications

| Location | Gap (mm) | Flush (mm) |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Door to fender | 4.0 ± 0.5 | 0.0 ± 0.5 |
| Door to door (B-pillar) | 4.0 ± 0.5 | 0.0 ± 0.5 |
| Hood to fender | 3.5 ± 0.5 | 0.0 ± 0.5 |
| Hood to headlamp | 3.0 ± 0.5 | 0.0 ± 0.5 |
| Trunk to body | 4.5 ± 0.5 | 0.0 ± 0.8 |
| Fender to bumper | 3.0 ± 1.0 | 0.0 ± 1.0 |

All gaps measured with graduated feeler gauge. Flush measured with flush gauge or Perceptron laser scanner.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Door alignment off | Hinge bolt loose, body variation, striker misaligned | Adjust hinge, check body reference points, realign striker |
| Hood gap uneven | Hood hinge adjustment, fender position, latch pulling | Adjust hinge, reposition fender, check latch engagement |
| Trunk seal gap | Hinge binding, gas strut wrong force, seal compression | Adjust hinge, verify strut part number, check seal cross-section |
| Trim fit issue | Clip location off, body variation, trim warped | Verify clip positions, measure body, replace trim if warped |
| Windshield adhesive failure | Surface contamination, primer missed, wrong adhesive | Clean surface, apply primer per spec, verify adhesive expiry date |
