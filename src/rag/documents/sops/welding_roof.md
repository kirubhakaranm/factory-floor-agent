# SOP-WLD-03: Roof and Closure Welding

**Station:** WLD-03-RCL | **Machine:** WLD-03-RCL-RBT01  
**Revision:** 2.4 | **Effective:** 2025-10-15

## Purpose

Welding of roof panel to body sides, plus installation of closure reinforcements (door hinge brackets, latch reinforcements).

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Nominal | Min | Max |
|-----------|---------|-----|-----|
| Weld current (roof) | 8.5 kA | 7.5 kA | 10.0 kA |
| Weld current (reinforcement) | 9.0 kA | 8.0 kA | 10.5 kA |
| Electrode force | 3.8 kN | 3.2 kN | 4.5 kN |
| Roof panel gap to body side | 0.5mm max | 0mm | 0.5mm |
| Laser brazing (if equipped) | 4.5 kW | 4.0 kW | 5.0 kW |

## Roof Panel Fit-Up

Roof panel alignment is critical for downstream paint appearance:
- Lateral position: ±0.5mm from centerline
- Longitudinal position: ±0.8mm from datum
- Flushness to body side: ±0.3mm
- Gap: 0.0–0.5mm (zero gap preferred for brazing)

Measure with laser gap/flush sensor before welding. Out-of-spec fit-up must NOT be welded — return to fixture adjustment.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Blow hole in roof weld | Zinc coating off-gassing, moisture | Extend squeeze time, add vent notch to electrode, dry parts |
| Lack of penetration | Low current, excessive gap, worn electrodes | Increase current, verify fit-up, dress electrodes |
| Electrode wear marks on roof | Excessive electrode force, contamination | Reduce force, clean electrode face, dress tips |
| Roof panel distortion | Excessive weld count, wrong sequence | Follow prescribed weld sequence, add cooling dwell |

## Model-Specific Notes

- **PE-SD100 (Sedan)**: Standard roof panel, 48 spot welds
- **PE-SV200 (SUV)**: Panoramic roof option — reinforced header rail, 56 spot welds, additional adhesive bond at rear
- **PE-CP300 (Compact)**: Shorter roof panel, 42 spot welds
