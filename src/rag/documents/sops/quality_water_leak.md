# SOP-QAT-02: Water Leak Test

**Station:** QAT-02-WLT | **Machine:** QAT-02-WLT-TST01  
**Revision:** 2.0 | **Effective:** 2025-08-15

## Purpose

Verify body sealing integrity by subjecting the vehicle to a controlled water spray simulating heavy rain conditions.

## Test Parameters

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Water pressure | 1.5 bar ± 0.2 bar |
| Nozzle distance | 300mm from body surface |
| Flow rate | 12 L/min per nozzle |
| Test duration | 4 minutes (full 360° rotation) |
| Water temperature | Ambient (15-30°C) |
| Number of nozzles | 48 (overhead and side jets) |

## Procedure

1. Close all windows, doors, trunk/hatch — verify all seals seated
2. Cover any intentional drainage paths for test (plugs at rear bumper drain holes)
3. Drive vehicle into spray booth — position on turntable
4. Start automated spray cycle — 4 minutes, vehicle rotates 360°
5. After spray: open all doors and inspect interior for water intrusion
6. Check critical areas with UV dye detection lamp (if dye added to water)
7. Record result per VIN — PASS or FAIL with location of leak

## Inspection Areas (Priority Order)

1. **Windshield** — adhesive bond perimeter, especially upper corners
2. **Rear glass** — adhesive bond, defroster connector pass-through
3. **Door seals** — primary and secondary lip seal, especially at corners
4. **Sunroof** (if equipped) — drain tubes, seal compression, guide rails
5. **Body seams** — roof-to-body side, A-pillar to cowl, rear quarter
6. **Drain plugs** — floor pan, rocker panel, rear compartment
7. **Harness grommets** — firewall pass-through, trunk pass-through
8. **Tail lamp and headlamp** — gasket seal

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Leak at windshield corner | Adhesive bead gap, primer missed, contamination | Mark location, remove and reseal windshield, re-test |
| Leak at door seal | Seal not fully seated, cut seal, door misaligned | Re-seat seal, replace if damaged, check door alignment |
| Leak at drain plug | Plug missing or not seated | Install/re-seat plug, verify correct part number |
| Leak at glass bond | Insufficient adhesive, surface contamination | Remove glass, clean, re-bond with correct primer and adhesive |
| Leak at harness grommet | Grommet not seated, wrong grommet size | Re-seat grommet, verify part number against body hole size |
| Leak at roof seam | Sealer bead broken, insufficient sealer thickness | Apply additional sealer, verify sealer gun output, re-test |

## Pass/Fail Criteria

- **PASS**: Zero water intrusion detected at any inspection point
- **FAIL**: Any water detected inside vehicle cabin — mark leak location with grease pencil, route to sealing rework station
- **NOTE**: All failures must be repaired and re-tested before vehicle proceeds to dyno
