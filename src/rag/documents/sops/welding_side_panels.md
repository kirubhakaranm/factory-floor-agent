# SOP-WLD-02: Side Panel Welding

**Station:** WLD-02-SDP | **Machine:** WLD-02-SDP-RBT01 / RBT02  
**Revision:** 3.0 | **Effective:** 2025-09-10

## Purpose

Spot welding of inner and outer side panels to underbody assembly, including A-pillar, B-pillar, and rocker panel joints.

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Nominal | Min | Max |
|-----------|---------|-----|-----|
| Weld current | 10.0 kA | 8.5 kA | 11.5 kA |
| Weld time | 14 cycles | 12 cycles | 18 cycles |
| Electrode force | 4.5 kN | 3.8 kN | 5.2 kN |
| Nugget diameter | 5.8mm | 4.8mm | 7.2mm |

## Critical Points

- B-pillar joint is structural (crash safety) — 100% monitoring via weld controller current/resistance curves
- Side panel to rocker joint has triple-thickness stack (1.2 + 1.5 + 0.8mm) — requires adaptive weld schedule
- A-pillar welds are visible after paint — spatter is cosmetic defect, zero tolerance

## Common Defects

| Defect | Cause | Corrective Action |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Crack at B-pillar joint | Residual stress, poor fit-up | Adjust fixture clamps, increase hold time, check gap with feeler gauge |
| Undercut on outer panel | Electrode misalignment, excessive force | Realign electrode, reduce force to nominal |
| Cold lap | Insufficient current, oxide layer | Increase current 0.5 kA, clean mating surfaces |
| Distortion of outer panel | Weld sequence error, excessive heat | Follow specified weld sequence, add cooling dwell between groups |

## Weld Count

- 186 spot welds per side (372 total per vehicle)
- 12 welds are safety-critical (B-pillar upper/lower, rocker front/rear) — these have 100% inline monitoring
