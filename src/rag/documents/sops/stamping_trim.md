# SOP-STP-03: Trim and Pierce Operation

**Station:** STP-03-TRM | **Machine:** STP-03-TRM-SRV01 / SRV02  
**Revision:** 2.0 | **Effective:** 2025-10-01  
**Owner:** Stamping Engineering

## Purpose

Standard procedure for trim, pierce, and flanging operations on formed panels at Station STP-03-TRM. This station produces final-dimensioned panels ready for welding.

## Operations Performed

1. **Trim**: Remove excess material (flash) from drawn panels
2. **Pierce**: Create holes for fasteners, locating pins, and drainage
3. **Flange**: Bend edges to final angle for hem joints and mating surfaces

## Operating Parameters

| Parameter | Nominal | Lower Limit | Upper Limit |
|-----------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Trim clearance | 8% of material thickness | 5% | 12% |
| Pierce punch-to-die clearance | 6% of thickness | 4% | 10% |
| Flange angle | per part spec | spec -1° | spec +1° |
| Cycle time | 30 sec | 22 sec | 40 sec |
| Burr height limit | 0.15mm | - | 0.30mm |

## Critical Quality Points

- **Burr height**: Measure every 25th part with burr gauge — must not exceed 0.30mm for safety (risk of cuts during assembly)
- **Hole position**: Check locating holes with go/no-go gauge — tolerance ±0.15mm
- **Edge quality**: No slug pull, no double-shear marks, no rollover exceeding 30% of thickness
- **Flange angle**: Verify with digital protractor — within ±1° of nominal

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Excessive burr (>0.30mm) | Dull trim steel, excessive clearance, misaligned punch | Resharpen or replace trim steel, check clearance with feeler gauge |
| Slug pull (slug sticks to punch) | Weak slug retention, oil on die, worn spring) | Clean die, replace spring stripper, apply slug retention compound |
| Oversized hole | Punch wear, incorrect punch-to-die clearance | Measure punch diameter, replace if undersize, adjust clearance |
| Incomplete trim | Short stroke, broken trim steel, part mislocated | Verify stroke length, inspect trim steel for fracture, check part locators |
| Edge cracking | Material too hard, excessive trim angle, sharp die radius | Verify material hardness, increase die radius, consider annealing |

## Die Maintenance Schedule

- Every 5,000 strokes: inspect trim steels and punch edges under magnification
- Every 20,000 strokes: resharpen trim steels (max 3 resharpenings before replacement)
- Every 50,000 strokes: full die inspection — all wear surfaces, springs, guide pins
