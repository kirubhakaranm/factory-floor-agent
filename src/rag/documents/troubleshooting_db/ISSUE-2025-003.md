# ISSUE-2025-003: Hydraulic Press Repeated Pump Failures — Station STP-01-PRS

**Date:** 2025-06-10 | **Severity:** Critical | **Status:** Resolved  
**Machine:** STP-01-PRS-HYP01 | **Downtime:** 14.2 hours total (3 events)

## Symptom
Three hydraulic pump failures on Press 1 within 6 weeks. MTBF dropped from 312 hours to 187 hours. MTTR increased from 1.8 hours to 3.2 hours due to parts unavailability.

## Timeline
- May 1: Pump failure #1 — replaced pump cartridge, 4.2 hrs downtime (waited 2.1 hrs for spare delivery)
- May 18: PLC module fault (unrelated) — 1.1 hrs
- May 29: Pump failure #2 (repeat) — 5.8 hrs downtime (needed crane, crane occupied on Line 2)
- June 10: Pump failure #3 — 3.5 hrs, servo motor alignment required specialist tech

## Investigation
1. Oil sample analysis: particle count ISO 21/19/16 — **above target of 18/16/13**
2. Fluid contamination: water content 800 ppm — **above 500 ppm limit**
3. Hydraulic filter: differential pressure at 3.2 bar — replacement threshold is 2.5 bar (overdue)
4. Maintenance log review: filter change was 200 hours overdue (missed during shift handover)

## Root Cause
Contaminated hydraulic fluid caused accelerated pump wear. Root of contamination traced to:
1. Overdue filter replacement allowed particle count to rise
2. Condensation in reservoir due to missing breather desiccant (dried out, not replaced)
3. High ambient temperature in May increased condensation rate

## Corrective Actions
1. Flushed hydraulic system completely and refilled with fresh ISO VG 46
2. Replaced all filter elements (return line, servo valve, kidney loop)
3. Replaced breather desiccant and installed moisture indicator
4. Stocked 2 spare hydraulic pump cartridges on-site (previously zero on hand)

## Preventive Actions
1. Added filter DP monitoring to SCADA alarm system — alert at 2.0 bar, auto-shutdown at 3.0 bar
2. Oil sampling frequency increased from 500-hour to 250-hour intervals for Press 1
3. Breather desiccant added to shift changeover checklist (visual check)
4. Cross-trained 2 additional technicians on Press 1 hydraulic system

## Cost Impact
- Downtime: 14.2 hours × $4,200/hr (lost production) = $59,640
- Parts and labor: $8,400
- Total: $68,040
- Preventive actions cost: $6,200 (spares + desiccant + training)
