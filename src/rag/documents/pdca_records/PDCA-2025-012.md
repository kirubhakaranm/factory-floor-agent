# PDCA Record: PDCA-2025-012 — Workstation Layout Review for New Options

**PDCA ID:** PDCA-2025-012 | **Date Opened:** 2025-09-20 | **Status:** Closed  
**Owner:** Manufacturing Engineering | **Station:** PNT-01-ECT, PNT-02-PRM  
**Linked Issue:** 5why_paint_adhesion.md (paint adhesion failure on PE-SV200 roof panel)

---

## PLAN

**Problem Statement:**  
13 PE-SV200 vehicles failed paint adhesion crosshatch test (ASTM D3359) at final inspection. Root cause: silicone contamination from adhesive tape backing placed on roof panel surface — an unapproved operator workaround caused by missing waste bin at workstation.

**Goal:** Eliminate silicone contamination risk on all vehicle models when adhesive-bonded components are installed. Prevent recurrence of unapproved handling workarounds due to workstation layout gaps.

**Scope:** All stations where adhesive-bonded or tape-backed components are installed. Immediate corrective action at WLD-03-RCL and PNT-01-ECT. Systematic review across all 15 stations.

**Success Criteria:**  
- Zero paint adhesion failures attributed to contamination at 30-day and 90-day review
- 100% of stations with adhesive processes have designated waste disposal within arm's reach
- Workstation layout review checklist completed for all new option introductions

---

## DO

**Actions Taken (2025-09-20 to 2025-10-05):**

1. Added waste bin at operator's immediate reach at WLD-03-RCL for adhesive backing disposal — completed 2025-09-21
2. Updated work instruction WI-WLD-03-004 to specify: backing tape must be placed directly into waste bin, never on vehicle surface — completed 2025-09-22
3. Reworked 13 affected PE-SV200 vehicles: sanded, re-primed, re-basecoated, re-clearcoated — completed 2025-09-25. Total cost: $15,600
4. Added silicone contamination check (water break test) to pre-primer checklist for all PE-SV200 units — effective 2025-09-23
5. Launched systematic workstation layout audit across all 15 stations — completed 2025-10-05

---

## CHECK

**30-Day Verification (2025-10-20):**
- Zero adhesion failures at 30-day review — confirmed
- Water break test passing 100% at PNT-01-ECT pre-primer check
- Workstation audit identified 2 additional stations (ASM-02-INT, ASM-03-FNL) with adhesive processes lacking dedicated waste disposal — both corrected by 2025-10-10

**90-Day Verification (2025-12-20):**
- Zero adhesion failures across 3 months of production
- No additional contamination-related rework events recorded
- Goal achieved: corrective action confirmed effective

---

## ACT

**Decision: STANDARDIZE**

New workstation layout requirements added to Manufacturing Engineering Standard ME-STD-007:
- All stations with adhesive-bonded or tape-backed components must have dedicated waste bin within 1 arm's reach
- New model/option introduction checklist now includes workstation layout review as mandatory gate
- Operator re-training on contamination risks added to new-hire onboarding for paint and body shop

**Lessons Captured:**
- Workstation layout must be reviewed when any new component or option is introduced — not just at initial station design
- Operator workarounds are a symptom of workstation design gaps, not operator error
