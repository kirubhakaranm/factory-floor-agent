| Zone | Function | Setpoint | Tolerance |
|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Zone 1 — Ramp-up | Ambient → 100°C | 120°C air | ±5°C |
| Zone 2 — Soak-1 | 100°C → 140°C | 150°C air | ±3°C |
| Zone 3 — Soak-2 | Hold at 140°C metal temp | 145°C air | ±3°C |
| Zone 4 — Cool-down | 140°C → 60°C exit | Ambient air | — |

## Maintenance Schedule

| Interval | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Daily | Verify zone temperature setpoints vs actual (all 4 zones), check burner flame status, inspect conveyor chain tension |
| Weekly | Clean recirculation fan inlet filters, check fresh air damper operation, verify IR pyrometer calibration at oven exit |
| Monthly | Full temperature uniformity survey with 12-channel data logger (Datapaq) — place loggers at 6 body positions, verify all positions reach 140°C ±5°C within cure window |
| Monthly | Inspect and lubricate conveyor chain (food-grade grease, high-temp rated to 200°C) |
| Monthly | Clean burner nozzles and check gas pressure (nominal 25 mbar) |
| Monthly | Inspect door seals on oven entrance/exit — replace if gap >5mm |
| Quarterly | Combustion analysis (CO, NOx, O₂) — adjust air-fuel ratio if CO >100 ppm |
| Quarterly | Inspect recirculation fan impellers for paint build-up — clean or replace if imbalance detected |
| Annually | Full burner overhaul, heat exchanger inspection, conveyor chain replacement |

## Common Failure Modes

1. **Zone temperature deviation** — actual >±5°C from setpoint. Cause: fouled burner nozzle, thermocouple drift. Action: clean nozzle, recalibrate thermocouple.
2. **Recirculation fan imbalance** — vibration, noise, reduced airflow. Cause: paint build-up on impeller. Action: clean impeller or replace.
3. **Conveyor chain elongation** — chain sag, skip, mistrack. Cause: lubrication failure or wear. Action: re-tension or replace chain links.
4. **Cure failure (under-cure)** — MEK rub test fails (<100 double rubs). Cause: temperature deviation, conveyor speed too high, body loading variation. Action: verify temperature uniformity survey, adjust conveyor speed.
5. **Door seal failure** — heat loss, temperature non-uniformity near entrance/exit. Cause: seal wear, mechanical damage. Action: replace door seal.
